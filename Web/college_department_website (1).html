About the department

A place to think, create, and innovate.

The Department of Computer Science and Engineering introduces students to the principles and practice of computing. Our learning environment encourages analytical thinking, creativity, teamwork, and responsible use of technology.

Through lectures, laboratory sessions, seminars, projects, and collaborative activities, students develop the knowledge and confidence to take on emerging technological challenges.

  • ✓Strong foundations in programming, algorithms, and software engineering.
  • ✓Practical exposure through projects, labs, and technical activities.
  • ✓Encouragement for research, innovation, and lifelong learning.

Facilities

Spaces designed for practical learning.

Our facilities support experimentation, teamwork, independent study, and the development of technical skills.

Computer Laboratories

Equipped workstations support programming practice, database exercises, web development, software testing, and computer network simulations.

Project & Innovation Space

Students can collaborate on prototypes, hackathons, coding challenges, mini-projects, and interdisciplinary technology activities.

Students can use technical books, journals, online learning materials, and dedicated study areas to support coursework and project research.
